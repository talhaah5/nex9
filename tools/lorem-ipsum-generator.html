Why designers still use Lorem Ipsum

Lorem Ipsum has been the standard placeholder text in publishing and design since the 1500s, scrambled from a passage of Cicero's Latin so it reads as plausible text without carrying any real meaning that would distract from layout, typography, and spacing decisions. It's still the fastest way to fill a mockup with realistic-looking copy before real content is ready.
