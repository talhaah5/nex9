How to use this word counter

Just start typing or paste text into the box above — every statistic updates live with no button to click. It's useful for checking essay or article length, meeting a strict character limit for a tweet or meta description, or estimating how long a piece will take readers to get through.

How the counts are calculated

Words are counted as whitespace-separated tokens. Sentences are counted by splitting on runs of . ! ?, and paragraphs by splitting on blank lines. Reading time is estimated at 225 words per minute, a commonly used average adult silent-reading speed, rounded up to the nearest minute so short texts still show at least one minute.

Privacy: This tool processes everything client-side in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never sent to any server.
