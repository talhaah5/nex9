Why nex9?

Most "free tool" sites bury a simple utility under trackers, pop-ups and multi-second ad-load delays. nex9 tools are static pages that do one job well: everything runs client-side in JavaScript, so your data never leaves your browser and the tools work even on a slow connection. No accounts, no email walls, no dark patterns.

Privacy: None of the tools on this site upload, log, or transmit the data you enter. Basic, privacy-respecting page-view analytics may be used to understand which tools are useful — see the privacy policy for details.
